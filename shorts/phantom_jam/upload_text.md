**Title:** Why Do Traffic Jams Appear Out of Nowhere? 🚗

**Description:**
No crash. No roadwork. So why is everyone stopped? 🤯
One driver taps the brakes for a second, and the slowdown grows into a wave that travels backwards for miles. Scientists call it a phantom traffic jam. The fix: keep a bigger gap and ease off instead of braking.
